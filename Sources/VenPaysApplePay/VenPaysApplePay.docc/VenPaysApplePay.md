# ``VenPaysApplePay``

Native iOS Apple Pay SDK for VenPays.

## Overview

VenPaysApplePay presents Apple Pay with PassKit, authorizes payment tokens against VenPays native APIs, and recovers status when outcomes are processing or uncertain.
